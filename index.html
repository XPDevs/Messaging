How this works: your 11-digit number is your ntfy.sh topic. Messages and call packets are delivered through ntfy.sh. Anyone who knows a topic number may be able to subscribe to it, so only share it with people you trust. Attachments are uploaded to ntfy.sh and may be temporary; received media is cached on this device when possible.
