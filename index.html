    How it works

    Your eleven digit number identifies your ntfy topic. Share it only with people you trust.

    Rich messages, photos, voice notes, files and location. Everything is delivered through ntfy.

    Vault Lock, encrypt text or a photo on your device with a passcode.

    Voice calls run entirely over ntfy: the audio is compressed to 8 kHz µ-law, packed into normal ntfy messages and rebuilt on the other side. No WebRTC, no STUN/TURN, no media server. Settings has a Call ringtone switch to call silently.

    Encrypted by default. Each device publishes an ECDH public key on the topic; both sides derive the same AES-GCM key and everything you send in a one to one chat (text, photos, voice notes, location, call audio) is sealed with it. Compare the safety number in Settings once and you know there is nobody in the middle.

    Catch-up and backup. On reconnect the app asks ntfy for what it missed instead of only listening for new messages, keeps history in IndexedDB, and can export or import a JSON backup.

    No microphone? Calls still work. Start or answer one and the app offers a listen-only call: you hear the other side, they cannot hear you, and both screens say so.

    Big files are split into chunks automatically, so an upload that would trip ntfy's size limit (HTTP 413) carries on in pieces and is stitched back together on the other side.
