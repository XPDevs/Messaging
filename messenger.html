    How it works

    Your 6-digit code is a topic on ntfy.sh. Share it (or the link) and anyone who enters it lands in this chat live. Add more codes to talk on other topics.

    Rich messages — photos, voice notes, files and location. Tap the buttons under the composer. Everything is delivered through ntfy.

    Reactions, replies, edit & delete — hover a message (or long-press on mobile) for actions. Replies quote the original; delete removes it for everyone.

    Vault Lock 🔒 — arm the lock, then send. Text or photo is encrypted on your device with a passcode, so even someone with your code sees only ciphertext.

    Urgent 🚨 — arm it to send a high-priority message that vibrates the other phone.
